  • A factory sells to the sectors within the jumps you pick. A sector counts fully at the spot and less with every jump: with 3 jumps, 100%, 75%, 50% and 25%.

  • The list is ranked by Expected Cr/h. The resources (closed loop) or inputs (buy inputs) in reach and the sector security are shown in their own columns, not ranked by. Energy cells follow the sector sunlight.
