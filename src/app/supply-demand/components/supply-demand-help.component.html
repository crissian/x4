Built capacity is what the stations are built to make and use per hour, with full inputs, their real workforce and sunlight. It is stable and decides what is worth building. Market now is what is on offer at save time. It changes within minutes and shows whether the capacity actually reaches the market.

The diagnosis puts the two together:

Market tight (price index 105% or more) Market calm or cheap
Built short (net below -2% of the larger flow) {{ kinds.short }} {{ kinds.covered }}
Built surplus (net above +2%) {{ kinds.starved }} {{ kinds.surplus }}

A ware built short with nothing on sale is {{ kinds.short }} as well: no stock covers the gap. When the capacity is balanced, the market decides: {{ kinds.tight }}, {{ kinds.balanced }} or {{ kinds.glut }}. Slight, moderate and severe tell how big the balance or the price gap is. Mined resources and wares used only for building are judged from the market only. Wares that building also uses, such as hull parts, keep their capacity diagnosis and are marked *, because their real demand is higher.
